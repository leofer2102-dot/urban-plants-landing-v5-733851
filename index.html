Context changes the dose

Indoor Pots vs. Outdoor Garden Beds

Indoor containers

Limited root volume, no rainfall flushing, and slower evaporation demand more precise dilutions and careful frequency control.

  • Smaller nutrient reservoir
  • Higher buildup risk
  • Measured, lighter applications

Outdoor beds

Greater soil volume, natural rainfall, weather shifts, and broader microbial diversity call for season-aware application timing.

  • Larger biological buffer
  • Natural leaching from rain
  • Seasonal feeding adjustments
